Item 34 · Difficulty Hard · Ability θ +0.82 ⏱ 04:12 remaining ✓ Tutor feedback enabled

A 68-year-old client is admitted to the medical unit with a suspected urinary tract infection. The client is confused, febrile, and hypotensive. For each intervention below, click to specify whether it is anticipated, contraindicated, or nonessential.

Nursing Intervention Anticipated Contraindicated Nonessential
Obtain blood cultures before starting antibiotics
Restrict oral fluids to 500 mL per day
Initiate isotonic IV fluid resuscitation per protocol
Reassess mental status and vital signs every 4 hours

Tutor Mode Rationale

Obtain blood cultures — Anticipated. Cultures must be drawn before the first antibiotic dose to preserve diagnostic accuracy, but they should never delay antibiotics in a hemodynamically unstable client.

Fluid restriction — Contraindicated. Sepsis requires aggressive isotonic fluid resuscitation; restriction worsens hypoperfusion and end-organ damage.

Reassessing every 4 hours — Nonessential. Frequent reassessment is expected, but a 4-hour interval is too infrequent for a client with suspected sepsis.
